FAQ

Before you ask…

What’s included in the rent?

Everything: 3 meals a day (veg & non-veg), high-speed WiFi, electricity, housekeeping and laundry. Plus TV in your room, a fridge on your floor, terrace gym, WFH lounge and rooftop access.

Booking, rent & notice?

No security deposit. Book with ₹5,000 — fully redeemable against your rent. At move-in you pay the booking amount plus your first month's rent; the security amount is partially refundable when you move out. 30-day notice. No brokerage — and no move-out fee, ever.

Is parking available?

Yes — dedicated 2-wheeler parking for your bike or scooter.

Where exactly is it?

Nallurhalli, Whitefield — walkable to the ITPL back gate, close to Divyashree Tech Park, EPIP Zone, Nallurhalli Metro and Vydehi Hospital.
